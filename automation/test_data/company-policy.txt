Northern Lighthouse — fictional company policy, version SM-2026-01.
This document is test data for RAG checks. All policies are fictional.

1. Paid leave
Each employee receives 23 working days of paid leave per year.
A leave request must be submitted at least 12 calendar days before leave starts.
The employee's direct manager approves the request.
Up to 6 unused working days may be carried over to the following year.
Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year.

2. Business travel
The daily travel allowance is KGS 2700 for each calendar day.
The company reimburses hotel accommodation up to KGS 8400 per night with a receipt.
An expense report must be submitted within 4 working days after returning.
The employee's direct manager and the finance department approve the trip.

3. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval.
Required availability hours are 11:00 to 16:00, Bishkek time.

4. Document scope
Bonus, parental leave and gym membership reimbursement policies are not covered.
